Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password. As you use the app, we store the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide. We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service to you. This includes displaying your expense history, generating category summaries, and allowing you to filter and review your spending. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using industry-standard algorithms before being saved. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Third-Party Services

Spendly uses Google Fonts to load typefaces for the interface. This means your browser may make a request to Google's servers when you visit the app. Beyond this, we do not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data brokers. Any future integrations will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
